The Agora: Philosophy Debates

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Agora: Philosophy Debates ("The Agora"), developed and operated by Bhernaurd Maghirang, collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use the iOS or Android app and related services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, The Agora may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account and identity information. When you sign in, the applicable identity provider may provide an account identifier and profile information. Android Google sign-in may provide a Google subject identifier, email address, email-verification status, display name, and profile-picture URL. iOS Sign in with Apple may provide Apple identity information and, when made available by Apple, a display name.
  • App-generated identifiers and session information. The app creates a random installation identifier used to associate an installation with app functionality, account sessions, subscription ownership, abuse prevention, and synchronization. Authentication access and refresh tokens are used to maintain signed-in sessions. Server-side security metadata may include hashed IP-address and user-agent values.
  • Debate and other user content. This can include debate messages and positions you submit, selected topics and philosophers, generated-question requests, Daily Challenge activity, and information needed to create replies, scores, reports, or shareable results.
  • AI response safety reports. If you use the in-app Report action on an AI-generated philosopher response, The Agora processes the selected report reason, a bounded copy of that specific AI response, the related debate/message identifiers, philosopher, debate type, account/installation association, and basic app platform/version metadata. The report does not intentionally include unrelated conversation messages.
  • Progress and app activity. This can include debate history, reports, scores, achievements, streaks, Daily Challenge progress, Ranked placement/rating/progress, feature interactions, and account-linked synchronization state.
  • Purchase and subscription information. The Agora processes store-provided subscription and purchase state needed to determine Pro access, such as product, plan or offer identifiers, trial/renewal state, expiration, transaction or order identifiers, and store ownership information. On Android, raw Google Play purchase tokens are used transiently for verification; the backend stores a SHA-256 fingerprint rather than the raw purchase token.
  • Notification information. If notifications are enabled or configured, Android may process an FCM push token, installation identifier, app version/build, timezone, selected/effective language, notification-permission state, reminder preference, and Daily Challenge completion information. Apple notification services may process the corresponding iOS notification information.
  • Language, device, and app configuration. This can include the app platform, app version/build, selected language or device-language setting, timezone, and settings required to provide localized or device-specific functionality.
  • Referral and affiliate information. If you arrive through a creator or referral link, The Agora may process the referral/affiliate code and attribution source so the referral can be credited. Android may also use Google Play Install Referrer for this purpose.
  • Diagnostics and security information. The Agora may process technical error, reliability, performance, security, fraud-prevention, and request metadata necessary to operate and protect the service.

2. How we use information

We use information described above to:

  • provide debates, philosopher responses, generated questions, scoring, Debate Reports, Daily Challenges, Ranked features, achievements, history, and cross-device account functionality;
  • review user-submitted AI response reports for safety, abuse prevention, model/output quality, and product improvement;
  • authenticate users, maintain secure sessions, and prevent account or subscription misuse;
  • verify App Store and Google Play purchases and maintain accurate Pro entitlements across supported devices;
  • deliver notifications that you enable and maintain your notification preferences;
  • localize the service and provide appropriate app/device behavior;
  • attribute eligible referrals and administer the affiliate program;
  • operate, troubleshoot, secure, improve, and understand the performance and reliability of The Agora; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Artificial intelligence processing

The Agora uses artificial intelligence to provide philosopher responses, debate feedback, scoring-related output, reports, insights, generated questions, and related educational functionality. Content needed to fulfill these requests, including relevant debate text and instructions, may be sent from The Agora's backend to Anthropic for processing through its API.

We use AI providers as service providers to perform functionality on our behalf. You should avoid including information in a debate that you do not want processed as part of the AI request. AI output can be inaccurate and should not be treated as professional, medical, legal, financial, or other expert advice.

4. Service providers and platform providers

We may provide information to service/platform providers when necessary to operate The Agora, including:

  • Anthropic for AI processing described above;
  • Google for Google sign-in, Google Play Billing and purchase verification, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Android platform services;
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases/subscriptions, offer redemption, and Apple notification/platform services;
  • hosting, database, email/notification, and infrastructure providers used to securely operate The Agora's backend and administrative services.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Data sharing and sale

The Agora does not sell personal information. We do not use personal information collected through the app for third-party behavioral advertising. Data may be transferred to service providers acting on our behalf as described in this policy, or when a transfer is necessary for a feature you intentionally use.

6. Security

The Agora uses HTTPS/TLS for network communication and applies technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. Sensitive account sessions are stored using platform-appropriate secure storage where implemented. Store purchase credentials are verified server-side before authoritative subscription access is granted. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain account-linked information while needed to provide the service, maintain account history/progress, secure the service, meet legitimate business or legal requirements, or until it is deleted in accordance with this policy. Some operational records may be retained for a limited period for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial reporting, or legal compliance.

Apple and Google separately control records held in your App Store, Apple, Google, or Google Play account under their own policies.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Agora account and associated account data from inside the app. You can also request deletion outside the app through our Account Deletion page.

Once account ownership is verified, The Agora deletes the account and associated user data except information that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, financial, dispute-resolution, or legal obligations. Account deletion does not itself cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription; subscription billing must be managed through the store where you subscribed.

9. Notifications and choices

You control notification permission through your device settings and can change supported reminder preferences in The Agora. You can also manage your App Store or Google Play subscription through the applicable store and delete your Agora account as described above.

10. Children

The Agora is not intended to be a child-directed service. If we learn that personal information has been collected in violation of applicable child-privacy law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise address it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated when required by applicable law or platform policy.
