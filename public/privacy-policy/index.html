The Agora: Learn Philosophy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Our current privacy practices for The Agora on supported platforms.

Overview

The Agora: Learn Philosophy (“The Agora,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an interactive philosophy application that allows users to learn philosophy, debate AI-generated representations of philosophers, participate in challenges and Ranked debates, explore philosophical questions and cases, build an AI-assisted philosophical profile through The Mirror, track progress, and access subscription features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Agora collects, how it is used, when it is shared, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to you.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Information We Collect

The Agora uses account authentication so your progress and supported app data can be associated with your account.

When you use Sign in with Apple, we may receive information from Apple such as:

  • A unique Apple account identifier used for authentication

The Agora does not receive your Apple Account password.

Where Sign in with Google is supported, we may receive information from Google such as:

  • A unique Google account identifier used for authentication

  • Authentication credentials and tokens required to securely maintain your account session and support account functions

The Agora does not receive your Google Account password.

We may also create and store an internal Agora account identifier associated with your account.

The app creates a randomly generated installation identifier.

We use this identifier to associate activity with a particular installation, including:

Please avoid including highly sensitive personal information in debate messages unless you are comfortable with that information being processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

The Mirror and Philosophical Profile

The Mirror is an AI-assisted feature that creates and updates a philosophical profile based on information you provide and qualifying philosophical activity within The Agora.

When you use The Mirror, we may process and store information such as:

  • Your responses to the Mirror questionnaire
  • Philosophical positions, reasoning, and other relevant content from qualifying debates
  • Relevant Daily Challenge activity
  • Relevant Ranked debate activity
  • Evidence selected from qualifying philosophical activity for use in a Mirror analysis
  • Evidence you exclude or otherwise manage within The Mirror
  • Philosophical dimensions and profile coordinates
  • Archetype information and related profile data
  • AI-generated summaries and interpretations
  • Patterns, tensions, changes, or recurring themes identified in qualifying philosophical activity
  • Philosophical connections and recommendations
  • Questions suggested for further reflection or debate
  • Historical Mirror snapshots
  • Comparisons between Mirror analyses over time
  • The language in which a Mirror analysis was originally generated
  • Translated versions of generated Mirror analysis when you view The Mirror in another supported language

The Mirror may generate inferences about your philosophical positions, values, reasoning patterns, or how those positions appear to change over time based on the content used in the analysis.

The Mirror is intended as an educational and philosophical reflection tool. It is not intended to provide a psychological, psychiatric, medical, or other clinical diagnosis.

We use Mirror information to:

  • Create your initial philosophical profile
  • Generate subsequent Mirror analyses
  • Identify patterns or changes across qualifying philosophical activity
  • Explain evidence that contributed to an analysis
  • Maintain your Mirror history
  • Compare current and previous philosophical profiles
  • Generate relevant questions or philosophical recommendations
  • Display generated Mirror analysis in supported languages

Mirror questionnaire responses and qualifying philosophical content may be transmitted to our AI service provider when necessary to generate a Mirror analysis.

When you view an existing Mirror in another supported language, the previously generated analysis may also be transmitted to our AI service provider for translation.

Translated versions of a Mirror are stored separately from the original analysis. Translation does not recalculate or intentionally alter the underlying philosophical profile, dimension values, evidence, archetype data, or historical snapshot.

A translated Mirror may be cached so that the same translation does not need to be generated again each time you return to that language.

Historical Mirror snapshots may be stored with your account so that you can review how your philosophical profile has developed over time.
