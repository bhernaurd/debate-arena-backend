Account Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Agora account and the app data associated with it, even if you no longer have the app installed.

If you still have the app

The fastest path is directly inside The Agora:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Account, choose Delete Account.
  3. Complete the Google or Apple identity confirmation shown by the app.

Deleting your Agora account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscription billing must be managed separately through the store where you subscribed.

If you cannot access the app

You can submit an external deletion request through The Agora website contact form.

  1. Open the contact form using the button below.
  2. Use the email address associated with your Agora sign-in whenever possible.
  3. In your message, write Account deletion request and state that you want your Agora account and associated app data permanently deleted.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion so another person cannot delete your account.
Open The Agora contact form

What the deletion request covers

Once account ownership is verified, the deletion request covers the Agora account and associated user data stored for that account, including app progress and account-linked activity such as:

  • debate history and synced debate data;
  • achievements and Daily Challenge progress;
  • Ranked identity, placement, rating, and related account progress;
  • Agora account sessions and account-linked preferences.

Data that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, financial, or legal obligations may be retained only to the extent required. Store transaction records controlled by Apple or Google are governed by those providers.

Need help?

If you have trouble submitting the request, use the Contact Us section on The Agora website and include the words Account deletion.
